 Can I create a Professional Voice Clone of someone else's voice? – ElevenLabs Skip to main content ElevenLabs Toggle navigation menu Back to website Documentation ElevenLabs Product Voice Cloning Can I create a Professional Voice Clone of someone else's voice? No. You can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice. Even with their consent, you cannot clone someone else’s voice. All Professional Voice Clones require a verification process to confirm that the voice belongs to you. If someone wants to share their voice with you, they can create and verify a Professional Voice Clone on their own account, then share it with you privately using a sharing link.
