Privacy Policy Reject Accept Home Accounts & Billing Accounts What type of plan do I have? Edited Friday, December 19 2025 Suno offers three plan types, two of which are paid subscriptions. The Free plan offers 50 credits that replenish daily. This is enough to make 10 songs per day! Pro is the entry option for paid subscriptions. With a Pro subscription, you receive an allotment of 2500 credits each month. Premier is our highest credit-per-month plan available. With a Premier plan, you receive 10,000 credits each month. You can compare the plans anytime by visiting suno.com/account Need more credits? Pro and Premier plans also have the option to purchase more credits. Once you're signed up, you'll see the Buy More Credits button in the area linked above. Pro and Premier users who reach their monthly allotment will now receive up to 50 credits per day after all monthly credits have been used!
