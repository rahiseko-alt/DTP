See terms Contact sales Safety / Runway's Usage Policy Trust & Safety Runway's Usage Policy Last updated March 6, 2026 At Runway, our mission is to help people tell stories by building the tools that can help them do so. Our usage policy — combined with our Standard Terms of Use and Enterprise Terms of Use — seeks to enable creative expression, while mitigating the risk of potential harm. Runway uses a combination of automated systems and internal human review to detect and block harmful content in user inputs and outputs, and prioritizes reports related to child safety. Runway reserves the right to suspend your account for any violation of this usage policy. If you believe your account has been wrongly suspended, you can appeal by emailing suspension@runwayml.com . Children's Safety We prohibit the following: Content that depicts, facilitates, or promotes child sexual abuse or the sexualization of children. We report any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) that we become aware of to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), which works with global law enforcement agencies around the world, and we indefinitely suspend all associated accounts. Content that facilitates minor grooming, including generating content designed to impersonate a minor Content depicting child abuse or minor participation in harmful or dangerous acts We also apply a stricter standard for potential harmful and inappropriate content (as outlined below) when a child is depicted. Violence & Gore We prohibit the following: Content that depicts, facilitates, or promotes terrorism or violent extremism Graphic violence or content that incites violence towards humans or animals Gore, such as dismemberment, beheadings, mutilations, and exposed organs/bones/muscle Sexually Explicit Content & Nudity We prohibit the following: Sexually explicit content, including sex toys and certain fetish content Adult nudity Attempts to create or modify non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) In some cases, we limit sexually suggestive content, including content featuring underwear or lingerie Hateful Conduct, Harassment, and Self-Harm We prohibit the following: Content that dehumanizes or promotes discrimination or violence on the basis of a protected attribute such as race, ethnicity, religion, gender, or sexual orientation Use of the service to create content with the aim of harassing, bullying, intimidating, defaming, or abusing others Content that depicts, facilitates, or promotes self-harm, including disordered eating Content that May Violate the Rights of Others We prohibit the following: Use of an image, video, or audio of another person without their permission Content that compromises the privacy of others Content that may violate intellectual property rights Attempts to create content in the style of a known, living artist Misleading Content We prohibit the following: Use of the service to defraud, scam, or deliberately mislead others Use of the service to impersonate an individual or entity, or to misrepresent your affiliation with an individual or entity Additional Character & Game Worlds Policies The policies outlined above apply to Runway Characters & Game Worlds. We also prohibit the following in these products: Characters based on the face or voice of a person under the age of 18. Characters or games that are intended to engage users under the age of 18, including narrative elements or visual styles intended for children Characters or games that are intended to provide medical, legal, or financial advice or mimic professional therapeutic or counseling interventions. This list is not exhaustive, and these policies will continue to evolve over time as our products and their use changes. If you encounter Runway content that you believe violates any of these policies, please report it immediately . You can learn more about your content ownership and commercial rights here .
