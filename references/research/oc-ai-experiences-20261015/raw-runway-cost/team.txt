Team is a plan for groups of two to nine people who create together in one Runway workspace. It includes everything in Max, plus shared projects, one pooled credit balance, frame-accurate commenting, shared agent skills and Agent Connectors. It is priced per seat: $69 a month, or $55 a month billed yearly. Can I upgrade from an individual plan? Yes. Upgrade to Team from your plan settings and invite your team. Your remaining credits come with you, and your billing date moves to the day you upgrade. How does the shared credit pool work? Each seat adds 6,900 credits a month to a single workspace balance. Anyone on the team can generate from it, there are no per-person allowances to manage and unused credits roll over for one month. Adding a seat adds its credits right away, and you can top up the pool at any time. Can I purchase additional credits? Yes. Top up anytime with credit packs, or turn on auto-refill so your team never runs dry. Purchased credits never expire. Can I change the number of seats? Yes. The workspace owner can add or remove seats at any time. Each new seat adds 6,900 credits to the pool right away. When should we consider Enterprise instead? Enterprise is the next step once you outgrow nine seats or one workspace, or need security and admin controls like SSO, roles and permissions, sharing policies and control over which third-party models your team can use. It also adds legal indemnification, dedicated support and onboarding. Talk to sales and we'll size it with you.
