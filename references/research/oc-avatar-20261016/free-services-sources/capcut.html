How to use the CapCut AI avatar generator

Step 1: Choose an AI avatar

Open CapCut and, in the editing interface, select "AI avatar" > "Avatar library" from the left panel. Browse the wide library of avatars and choose the one that best fits your content.  

Step 2: Add speech

Next, add speech for your avatar. You can either type your script manually or upload an audio file to match the avatar.

Step 3: Generate an AI avatar

Once everything is ready, click "Generate" and CapCut will create the avatar video with your chosen audio. You can further customize it with captions, custom stickers, filters, and more.  

Step 4: Export the AI avatar

When you're satisfied, click "Export" and adjust video settings like format, framerate, bitrate, and codec. Finally, hit "Export" again to save your finished video.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI avatar generator?

An AI avatar generator is a smart tool that creates digital versions of people, commonly known as avatars. With CapCut's AI avatar generator, you can design distinctive, non-anime characters that let you creatively showcase your personality and style.

What can I use an AI avatar generator for?

An AI avatar generator is ideal for creating videos for marketing, education, social media, and personal branding. It helps you design talking avatars that deliver messages in a visually captivating way. You can explore more creative video options through CapCut's AI video maker to elevate your storytelling.  

Can I customize the character's appearance in CapCut?

You can't fully change physical traits such as hair or clothing style, but CapCut still offers plenty of ways to customize your avatar. You can tweak colors, include accessories, and change the background to design a personalized look that matches your style and creative vision.  

What kind of voices can my AI avatar video have?

AI avatar generators like CapCut offer a wide variety of voice options, including different tones, accents, and even multiple languages. You can choose a voice that best fits your brand style or audience preference, making the content more relatable and engaging. This flexibility ensures your videos sound professional and authentic. Plus, with CapCut's AI video maker, you're not limited to avatar videos—you can also use AI templates and instantly turn text into full videos.  

Can I export high-quality videos from an AI avatar generator?

Yes, AI avatar generators allow you to export videos in 8K, ensuring sharp visuals and clear audio. With CapCut, you can adjust export settings such as format, frame rate, and bitrate to suit your needs. This makes it easy to produce polished, professional-quality videos ready for any platform. Additionally, with CapCut's AI video maker, you can cinstantly turn text into full videos, expanding your creative possibilities beyond avatar content.  

How do I add captions and effects after generating an AI avatar?

Continue editing the avatar video with captions, effects, cover settings, and other available tools before sharing. Review the complete composition rather than exporting the avatar as an isolated clip.

How does creating a talking AI avatar from a photo work?

Upload a photo and CapCut can animate it into a talking avatar. Use a clear source image, then review facial movement and speech timing before export.

How can I turn my own video into a reusable AI avatar?

Turn the uploaded video into a talking avatar that can be reused in later content. Review the generated avatar before using it across multiple projects.

How do I add speech to an AI avatar?

Speech can be added by typing a script or uploading an audio file, depending on the options shown in the current workflow. Play the full result once to check the avatar's speech timing.

Does an AI avatar generator synchronize lip movement with speech?

Lip-sync is included for the speech selected in the talking-avatar workflow. Preview the full narration and correct the script or audio timing if the mouth movement does not align as expected.
